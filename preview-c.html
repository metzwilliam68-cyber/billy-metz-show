Meet Your Host

Hey, I'm Billy.

Broken boy. Mended man. Follower of Jesus.

I did the thing you're told will finally make you somebody. Top of my field, money handled, the results to prove it. From the outside, it looked like I'd arrived.

Then I found out what success can actually give you: financial stability. And nothing else. It couldn't hand me worth. It couldn't tell me who I was. It couldn't make me loved.

At Jesus' baptism, before a single miracle, the Father said: “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.” Beloved first. Works second. I live from that same word now: I'm a deeply beloved son of the Most High God.

That's why I do this show. Not to perform, not to prove. Just to sit down with successful men and talk honestly about what worth really is.

The Journey

Faith isn't a straight line up.

In Acts, Saul is stopped on the road (Acts 9), an official reads Isaiah in his chariot (Acts 8), a Roman officer is already praying when Peter arrives (Acts 10). Different men, different moments, different paths.

A winding road is still a road home. Wherever you are on it, you're not behind.
