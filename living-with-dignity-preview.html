Our Mission
To help men know they are worthy of God's love in Christ Jesus, and to disciple them to live, lead, and work from that identity, not for it.
The Problem

Men are drowning quietly.

For years, leading men's Bible studies, I've seen the same thing again and again: successful men who are struggling, with nowhere to name it and no way to measure it.

Problem 01

A discipleship gap

Most men aren't being discipled. There's no place to feel what they need to feel, talk honestly about what's really going on, and grow closer to Jesus together. Sunday isn't enough, and most men never get past small talk.

Problem 02

No measuring stick

There's no assessment, no metric to help a man see what's going on inside. We measure revenue, reps and results, but not the soul.

When a man says, “I'm struggling here. I'm drowning here,” what is that? What's really there?
Our answer:Honest discipleship in brotherhood, and a measuring stick that shows each man where he is today and where he needs to go.

The Dignity Year

Twelve months. Six domains. One thread.

A year of formation for the man whose worth has been riding on his performance. Each domain gets two months: the first for conviction, the truth heard and believed; the second for practice, the truth lived until it shows.

What a man leaves with

  • A name he did not earn: worth settled in sonship, off the scoreboard for good.
  • Two scores and a story: his Week 0 and Week 48 results, and the distance between them.
  • A trained emotional life: he can name an emotion, read it, pause, and lead with it.
  • A rule of life: a one-page plan in his own words: his practices, his triad, his guardrails.
  • Brothers: an alumni triad that keeps meeting monthly. Nobody grows alone.

What We Believe

Beloved first. Works second.

Success can give a man stability. It can't give him a name. That was settled at the cross, and spoken over every son who follows Jesus.

Identity before performance

Your worth was given, not earned. You were loved before you did anything.

“See what kind of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God.” 1 John 3:1

Adoption, not orphanhood

Men don't need more pressure. They need to know whose they are.

“You have received the Spirit of adoption as sons, by whom we cry, ‘Abba! Father!’” Romans 8:15

Growth is a winding road

Faith moves forward, sideways, back and forward again. Wherever you are, you're not behind.

Acts 8, 9, 10 & 16

No man grows alone

Brotherhood isn't optional. We grow when we're known.

“As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another.” Proverbs 27:17
Our Story

Why I started Living With Dignity.

I did the thing you're told will finally make you somebody. Top of my field, money handled, the results to prove it. And I found out success can give you financial stability, and nothing else. It couldn't tell me who I was.

Then I learned what Jesus heard before a single miracle: “This is my beloved Son.” Beloved first. Works second. That changed everything.

For years I've led men's Bible studies and seen the same thing again and again: successful men, quietly empty, trying to earn what's already been given. Living With Dignity exists so no man has to carry that alone.

Billy Metz, Founder
