Why We Exist

Men are drowning quietly.

For years, teaching men's Bible studies, I've seen the same thing again and again: successful men who are struggling, with nowhere to name it and no way to measure it.

Problem 01

A discipleship gap

Most men aren't being discipled. There's no place to feel what they need to feel, talk honestly about what's really going on, and grow closer to Jesus together.

Problem 02

No measuring stick

There's no assessment, no metric to help a man see what's going on inside.

When a man says, “I'm drowning here,” what is that? What's really there?
The Dignity Year

Twelve months. Six domains.

A discipleship and teaching curriculum for local churches. Men meet monthly for teaching and weekly in triads, with the Word, emotion training and one practice each week.

Months 1–4

The Name You Were Given

  • Identity & Worth
  • Surrender & Trust
Months 5–8

The Inner Life

  • Emotional Honesty
  • Work & Ambition
Months 9–12

The Life That Flows From It

  • Presence & Family
  • Cultural Resistance

Measured, not assumed: every man takes the Living in Dignity assessment at the start, at month 6 and at month 12, and finishes the year with the evidence that he's grown.

Our Story

Why I started Living With Dignity.

I did the thing you're told will finally make you somebody. Top of my field, the results to prove it. And I found out success can give you financial stability, and nothing else. It couldn't tell me who I was.

Then I learned what Jesus heard before a single miracle: “This is my beloved Son.” Beloved first. Works second.

I teach men's Bible studies, and I host The Billy Metz Show. Living With Dignity exists so no man has to carry this alone.

Billy Metz, Founder
