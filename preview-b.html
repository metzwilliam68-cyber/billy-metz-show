Your Host

Meet Billy.

Broken boy. Mended man. Follower of Jesus.

Billy did the thing you're told will finally make you somebody. Top of his field, money handled, the results to prove it. From the outside, it looked like he'd arrived.

Then he found out what success can actually give you: financial stability. And nothing else. It couldn't hand him worth. It couldn't tell him who he was. It couldn't make him loved.

At Jesus' baptism, before a single miracle, the Father said: “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.” Beloved first. Works second. Billy lives from that same word: he is a deeply beloved son of the Most High God.

That's why he hosts the show. Not to perform, not to prove, but to sit down with successful men and redefine what worthiness really is.

The Journey

Faith isn't up and to the right.

In Acts, Saul is stopped on the road (Acts 9), an official reads Isaiah in his chariot (Acts 8), a Roman officer is already praying when Peter arrives (Acts 10). Different men, different moments, different paths.

A winding road is still a road home. Wherever you are on it, you're not behind.

Success gave me stability. Sonship gave me a name.
