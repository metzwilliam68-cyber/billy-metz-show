About Billy

At 22, I took an all-commission Medicare job and struggled. I was closing 2 or 3 deals a week.

Then I met Marcel Brunel. He didn't teach me a new script. He taught me to regulate my own emotions and to respect and treat my customers with dignity, and he paired that with the sales skills I needed. IQ plus EQ.

Within six months, I was closing 10 to 15 deals a week, about five times as many. I became the #1 rep out of 77, and I was the newest person in the agency. My client retention was 92% when the company average was around 50%.

That's the proof emotions aren't irrelevant. They drive relationships, results and revenue:

  • Relationships: 92% retention
  • Results: #1 out of 77
  • Revenue: five times the closes

That's why Marcel and I wrote Dignity in Sales. EQ matters just as much as IQ, and IQ plus EQ equals competence.

Ever since, I've been passionate about helping companies bring IQ and EQ together. The IQ side is the knowledge, skills and application of sales. The EQ side is the reason reps don't use what they know: why they disqualify too early, why they don't prospect up, why they burn out, and why veterans aren't performing like they did two years ago. Most training only teaches the what. We help reps with the why, and that's where the results come from.
